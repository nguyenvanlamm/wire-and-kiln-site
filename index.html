A cloisonné puzzle for iPhone

Wire and Kiln

Trade enamel across the wire. Fire the piece. Tap the gold dot between two cells and they swap their enamel: fill each cell with its own colour, and keep the frit dish from running over.

How it plays

Three moves to learn, eighty pieces to fire

  1. Trade

    Tap the solder dot on a wire. The two cells on either side trade their enamel.

  2. Mind the dish

    Enamel that does not fit falls into a small frit dish. If the dish runs over, the piece is spoilt.

  3. Fire

    A cell full of its own enamel is fired and locks, and becomes a wall that shapes every trade after it.

What's inside

A workshop catalogue to fill

80 pieces

Five collections: Small Dishes, Trinket Boxes, Vases, Pendants and Screens. Every piece is checked by the game's own solver; its par is the fewest trades it takes.

Two bench tools

The Spatula takes back a trade. The Loupe shows the next good one.

A cabinet

Every fired piece takes its place on a shelf. Fire one at par and it earns a hallmark.
