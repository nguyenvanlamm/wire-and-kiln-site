A cloisonné puzzle: trade enamel across the wire, then fire the piece.

How to play

Tap the gold solder dot on the wire between two cells: they trade their enamel. Enamel that does not fit falls into the frit dish, and if the dish runs over, the piece is spoilt. A cell full of its own enamel (the colour on its chip) is fired and locks. Fire every cell to finish the piece. The Spatula takes back a trade, the Loupe shows the next good one, and Start over begins the piece again.
