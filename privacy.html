Wire and Kiln Privacy Policy

Wire and Kiln home · last updated 27 November 2026

Wire and Kiln is a puzzle game. It does not ask who you are, and it does not sell any data about you.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: the pieces you have fired, your best trade count on each, and your Spatula and Loupe stock.
  • Your settings: whether sound is on.
  • Whether you have bought the East Gallery or the West Gallery, and which purchases have already been delivered.

This is stored on your device only. Deleting the app removes it.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Purchases

The first collection, Small Dishes, is free. Purchases (East Gallery, West Gallery, Bench Set) are handled entirely by the App Store under its own privacy policy. We receive no payment details; the game only learns from the store which purchases exist.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
